## Prisma versions

- **Prisma 7.1 and later** are supported, and tested on the oldest and the newest in CI.
- **Prisma 6** is not: it has no SQL comments, and its query engine runs the driver adapter outside
  the request's async context, so statements cannot be tied to their request.
- **Prisma 8** needs its own integration, built on its new middleware, once it is stable.

## Databases

Through Prisma's own driver adapters, tested in CI with the same checks on each: operations and
their lines, SQL and parameters, batch and interactive transactions, errors, hidden secrets and the
N+1 warning.

- **PostgreSQL**, with `@prisma/adapter-pg`, tested on 18. Raw SQL sent with `pg` is recorded too,
  through `profilePool()`.
- **MySQL** and **MariaDB**, with `@prisma/adapter-mariadb`, tested on MySQL 8.4 and MariaDB 11.4.
- **SQLite**, with `@prisma/adapter-better-sqlite3`.

## Node servers

The [preload](/docs/guides/node) works with any framework that serves through Node's `http` module.
CI runs the same requests, with Prisma against PostgreSQL, in each of these:

- **Express 4.18 and later**, 5 included, tested on 4.18.0, 5.0.0 and the newest. Before 4.18,
  Express's body parser drops the request's async context: a route that reads a body has its
  queries in a background profile instead.
- **Fastify**, tested on 4.26.0, 5.0.0 and the newest.
- **Koa 3**, **Hapi 21**, **NestJS 12** on Express, and **Hono 4** on `@hono/node-server`.

## Node

Node 22.19+ or 24.5+, tested on 22.19, the newest 22 and the newest 24. The preload gives your app's
imports of the profiler its real code through Node's synchronous module hooks: before those versions,
Node ignores the conditions such a hook adds on `import` and throws on them in `require()`. Node 20,
whose support ended in April 2026, lacks those hooks altogether.
